Lead / Senior AI/ML Engineer on GCP - healthcare payer: LangGraph, Python services, FHIR, RAG, HIPAA

6-week plan plus an optional add-on week for a remote contract role at the Lead or Senior level. Weekdays ~2-3 h, weekends one long block for the workbook exercises. Checkboxes save in this browser.

Overview

One remote contract posting for both a Lead and a Senior AI/ML Engineer on GCP at a healthcare payer, 12+ years of experience. The work is production LLM applications, RAG pipelines and agent frameworks (LangGraph first) on Vertex AI, now documented as Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform: Model Garden, endpoint deployment, Vector Search, Workbench, with Cloud Run, Pub/Sub and Cloud Functions around it, written as advanced, tested, containerized Python (asyncio, FastAPI, Pydantic, gRPC). The domain is payer workflows (prior authorization, claims, appeals, member engagement) on FHIR, X12 and ICD-10/CPT, with HIPAA and PHI handling inside the AI pipeline. Nice-to-haves: Terraform and GCP IAM/VPCs, PEFT/LoRA, evals in CI, and the Professional ML Engineer or Cloud Architect certification. This plan builds on the shared core and does not reteach CI/CD, Terraform or production operations.

Honest fit note. Two hard filters that study does not clear: "12+ years of software/AI engineering" and "3+ years directly building, deploying, and maintaining production-grade LLM applications, RAG pipelines, or autonomous agent frameworks". Your 26 years are infrastructure and DevOps years; do not stretch either number. Payer experience is a strong preference and you have not worked at a payer: learn the vocabulary and the CMS rule, and say so plainly. Your strongest card is regulated-environment operations (federal and SOX controls, audit trails, least privilege, approval gates), which is exactly what the HIPAA Security Rule asks for. Senior is the realistic target once the portfolio is deployed and you can walk its code line by line; apply for Lead only if the team needs someone to own platform, security and delivery for the AI work. Ask the recruiter whether the 3+ years is a hard cutoff for Senior, and which payer workflow the team starts with.

Portfolio project: prior-authorization assistant on GCP

One public repo, synthetic data only, that touches every must-have in the posting:

  • Data: Synthea patients in a Cloud Healthcare API FHIR store, and synthetic medical policies in a payer's coverage-criteria style, large enough that retrieval is a real problem. ICD-10-CM codes; procedures by HCPCS Level II or a generic label, never CPT descriptors. Week 3, week 5.
  • Intake: FastAPI accepting a PAS-shaped Bundle, validated with Pydantic, published to Pub/Sub. Weeks 1-3.
  • Agents: LangGraph with intake, policy retrieval, a criteria-check agent using tool calls against the FHIR store, and a drafting agent that cites every criterion; checkpointed in Postgres so a case can wait days. Week 4.
  • Human in the loop by design: the system may only recommend approval; every denial, low-confidence and missing-information case goes to a reviewer through an interrupt, and the decision and reason are stored. Week 4.
  • Clock: every case records its CMS-0057-F deadline (72 hours expedited, 7 calendar days standard) and a dashboard shows time remaining; no case closes without a specific denial reason. Week 1.
  • Retrieval: structure-aware chunks, Vector Search hybrid dense plus sparse, the ranking API, metadata filters by line of business and effective date. Week 5.
  • Services: FastAPI edge, gRPC between internal stages, Cloud Run with separate service accounts and no public ingress except the edge. Week 2.
  • PHI handling: a PHI data-flow map, de-identification for every copy that leaves the FHIR store, Data Access audit logs, an "if this were real" BAA section. Week 6.
  • Evals in CI: recall@k, citation support, criteria accuracy, and a hard check that no denial bypasses review. Week 6.
  • Small model and infrastructure: a LoRA comparison for ICD-10-CM extraction (add-on) and Terraform for the whole setup applied with Workload Identity Federation (role 6).

Keep it cheap: small endpoints undeployed after each session and a make destroy. No real PHI, no employer specifics, no real payer's policies.
