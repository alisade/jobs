## Description

Our client is a leading enterprise data and AI platform, trusted by thousands of organizations worldwide.
Internally, they're taking on a big project: moving their first-party business applications off a major
third-party CRM platform and onto systems they build and own. We're looking for strong backend engineers
to join the team building the foundation beneath this: the platform layer that will power agentic apps and
workflow automation across the company.

This is foundational work, not feature work. Early on you'll spend a lot of time at the whiteboard designing
architecture, data models, and service boundaries. Then you'll build the backend services, infrastructure,
and databases that everything else will run on. If you like hard system design problems and building
platforms other engineers will depend on, this role is for you.

## Tasks and deliverables

- Take part in early architecture and whiteboarding sessions to design the platform layer for agentic apps and workflow automation.
- Design and build backend services in Python and TypeScript that will replace capabilities currently provided by a third-party CRM platform.
- Design data models and database schemas for core business entities, with scale, integrity, and migration in mind.
- Build and maintain the supporting infrastructure: containerized services, deployment pipelines, and the systems that keep everything reliable in production.
- Build workflow automation primitives and service interfaces (APIs, events, integrations) that product teams and agentic apps can build on.
- Contribute to migration planning so internal apps can move off the legacy platform safely.
- Use AI tools in your daily development workflow to move faster without cutting corners on quality.

## Required experience

- Senior-level backend engineering experience designing and building complex, distributed backend systems in production.
- Strong production experience with Python for backend services.
- Strong production experience with TypeScript on the backend (Node.js).
- Solid infrastructure experience: Docker, Kubernetes, CI/CD pipelines, and running services reliably in production.
- Deep database experience, especially PostgreSQL: schema design, query optimization, and data modeling for complex business domains.
- Experience designing and building backend services and APIs (REST and/or GraphQL) that other teams depend on.
- Comfort with system design and whiteboarding: able to reason through trade-offs and explain architectural decisions clearly.
- Experience with Go or Java.

## Nice to have

- Hands-on use of AI coding tools (e.g., Claude Code, Cursor, Copilot) as part of a daily development workflow.
- Experience with workflow automation or orchestration systems, or with agentic/LLM-based applications.
- Experience with messaging and caching systems such as Kafka, RabbitMQ, or Redis.
- Familiarity with CRM data models (e.g., Salesforce) or experience migrating off a CRM platform.
- Frontend experience (React) is a plus, though not a focus of this role.
