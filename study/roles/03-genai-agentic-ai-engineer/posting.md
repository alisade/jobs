## Overview

We are looking for an experienced AI Engineer to build and deliver production-grade AI/ML and Generative AI
solutions. The ideal candidate combines strong software engineering fundamentals with hands-on experience
developing AI applications, leveraging AI coding assistants, and implementing modern agentic development
workflows.

This role requires someone who can work across Python and TypeScript/React, navigate multiple codebases and
technology stacks, and independently drive technical solutions from concept through production.

- Design, develop, and deploy production-grade AI/ML and Generative AI applications.
- Own AI engineering initiatives end-to-end, from technical design through implementation, testing, deployment, and optimization.
- Leverage AI coding assistants and agentic development workflows to improve engineering productivity and accelerate development.
- Develop full-stack applications using Python, TypeScript, and React.

- Work across different codebases, technology stacks, and AI platforms while maintaining development efficiency and quality.

- Mentor engineers and contribute to technical decisions, coding standards, and engineering best practices.
- Demonstrate strong ownership, prioritization, and execution of delegated technical tasks.
- Apply responsible AI principles related to bias, safety, reliability, and ethical AI development.
- Participate in Agile development practices and utilize tools such as Jira and Confluence.
- Communicate complex AI/ML concepts clearly to both technical and non-technical audiences.
