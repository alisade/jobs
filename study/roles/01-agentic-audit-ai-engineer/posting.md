# Posting: AI Engineer, Agentic Audit Controls Testing

Saved 2026-11-13. Text as received.

---

Seeking three hands-on AI Engineers to build an agentic AI application that automates audit controls testing. The project uses Azure for compute and storage and Google Gemini for the LLM, with Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform in scope.

## Responsibilities

- Design, build, and deploy AI agents that retrieve information, call tools, and execute multi-step workflows with human review where needed.
- Build the orchestration layer that lets agents execute reusable skills, manage context and state, recover from errors, and track execution.
- Integrate agents with enterprise APIs, document repositories, databases, and governance, risk, and compliance systems.
- Work with audit, risk, and business stakeholders to automate evidence collection, document review, population and sample analysis, control validation, and exception identification.
- Make results traceable to supporting evidence. Implement evaluations, logging, monitoring, access controls, and review checkpoints. Measure accuracy, consistency, cost, and performance against defined benchmarks.

## Required experience

- Hands-on experience building and deploying agentic applications that execute multi-step workflows.
- Strong Python and/or TypeScript skills, including backend development and API integration.
- Experience implementing tool calling, structured outputs, workflow orchestration, agent state management, and reusable agent capabilities.
- Experience grounding and evaluating LLM outputs, handling exceptions, and securing applications that interact with enterprise data.
- Ability to translate business requirements into working software and communicate with technical and business stakeholders.

## Preferred experience

- Direct experience building AI harnesses that load and execute reusable skills.
- Applying agentic AI to internal audit, SOX/ITGC testing, compliance assessments, or other controls testing.
- Familiarity with control design, operating effectiveness, evidence sufficiency, sampling, and exception documentation.
- Experience integrating with GRC, identity, ticketing, or ERP systems and implementing scoped permissions, approval gates, sandboxed execution, or auditable agent activity.
