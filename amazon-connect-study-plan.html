Lead Amazon Connect / AWS Solution Architect - Enterprise Help Desk Transformation

9-week plan plus an optional add-on week, built for six Amazon Connect postings and an AI contact center skills list. Weekdays ~2-3 h, weekends one long block for the workbook exercises. Checkboxes save in this browser.

Overview

Connect fundamentals are skipped. The plan goes to lead-architect depth on what changed since 2024 and on everything the six postings ask for that is not already on the resume: telephony at SIP depth, legacy IVR platforms, Lex and Bedrock, Contact Lens, DevOps for Connect, Java and the federal context.

Names changed in 2026. Amazon Connect is now branded Amazon Connect Customer (April 2026; API, CLI, AWS::Connect::* and the AWS/Connect metrics namespace are unchanged). Contact Lens is documented as conversational analytics, Amazon Q in Connect as agent assist / AI agents (APIs still wisdom/qconnect), and forecasting, capacity planning and scheduling as forecasting and agent scheduling. This guide uses the new names and mentions the old ones, because interviewers and postings still use both. Voice ID reached end of support on 2026-05-20 and Chime SDK PSTN audio entered maintenance mode on 2026-09-29.

Why 9 weeks plus an optional add-on

  • The original Perficient posting alone fits in 3 weeks for someone who ran Connect from inside AWS: you would only need the 2024-2026 delta (AI agents, Bedrock, newer Connect features) and ServiceNow.
  • Six postings plus an AI skills list widen the target. Each added area is one week because each is a separate interview loop: contact center design (W2), telephony at SIP depth with BYOC and Chime SDK (W3), Cisco/Avaya/Five9/Genesys hands-on knowledge (W4, the recruiter's top rejection reason), Lex/Bedrock/RAG (W5), Contact Lens (W6), DevOps plus Java (W8) and federal/identity (W9).
  • Fundamentals are not taught again: no week on what a queue or a Lambda is. Days start at the decision a lead makes (budgets, failure modes, trade-offs) and end in a graded exercise.
  • Weekdays are ~2-3 h of reading and small builds; the weekend block is for the 43 workbook exercises (about 2-3 h each, design ones graded by rubric). That is roughly 20-25 h per week, sustainable alongside a current contract.
  • Week 10 is optional: outbound campaigns, forecasting and agent scheduling in depth, cost modelling and DR game days. Take it if a client conversation turns that way.
  • If time is short: W1, W5, W7, W9 plus the Interview prep tab cover the Perficient core in 4 weeks; add W4 for P2, W8 for P3/P4/P6, W9 for P5.

ThuLambda in flows, and what IaC really covers

Synchronous invoke is capped at 8 s (Connect retries errors inside that window); async mode allows up to 60 s with a Wait block and Load Lambda result; a chain of Lambdas is capped at 20 s; responses up to 64 KB as STRING_MAP or JSON. Then the IaC map: AWS::Connect::* types, CDK (L1 only for Connect), Terraform aws_connect_*.
