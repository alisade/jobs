Design exercise: grade.sh reports 0/0 tests, so there is no automated row; all 100 points are judged.

| # | Criterion | Points | How to judge |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Channels, versions and skew | 12 | Full: channel per environment with a reason (dev/stg ahead of prod, e.g. Regular for dev/stg and Stable for prod, Extended considered for PCI), explicit version flow and soak, the Kubernetes skew policy (nodes may lag the control plane by a bounded number of minors) and a plan to close prod-blue's 1.32 nodes under a 1.33 control plane before 1.34. | 2 | Windows and exclusions | 12 | Full: windows moved to staffed hours (Tue-Thu 14:00-20:00 UTC) for prod, exclusions for quarter-end, Black Friday and year-end with the right scopes and their limits (a no-upgrades exclusion is capped in length; no-minor scopes can run longer), notifications (Pub/Sub cluster notifications) driving a calendar and a pre-check job. | 3 | Node pool strategy per workload class | 18 | Full: separate strategy for pool-default (surge with maxSurge > 1 and maxUnavailable 0, or blue-green with batches and soak for the riskiest bump), pool-stateful (blue-green with small batches and long soak, or manual one-node-at-a-time with a gate on search cluster health, never in parallel with another pool), batch pool, and sequencing pools one at a time.
