# Laptop and equipment refresh

Company laptops are refreshed every 3 years for standard roles and every 2 years for engineering and
data science roles. The service desk opens a refresh ticket automatically 30 days before your device
reaches its refresh date.

Lost or stolen laptops must be reported to the service desk and to security within 24 hours so the
device can be wiped remotely. Monitors, keyboards, and docking stations can be requested once per
refresh cycle through the IT catalog.

Old devices must be returned within 14 days of receiving the replacement; unreturned devices are
charged to the cost center.
