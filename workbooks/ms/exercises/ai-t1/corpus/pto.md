# Paid time off (PTO)

Litware employees accrue paid time off every pay period. Full-time staff accrue 1.67 days of PTO per
month, which is 20 days per calendar year. Part-time staff accrue PTO in proportion to their scheduled
hours. PTO requests go through Workday and need manager approval at least two weeks in advance for
absences longer than three consecutive days.

Carryover: you may carry over a maximum of 5 unused PTO days into the next calendar year. Any balance
above 5 days is forfeited on January 1 unless your country requires payout by law. PTO is not paid out
on resignation in the United States except in states where the law requires it.

Public holidays are separate from PTO and are published each December for the following year. Sick
leave is also separate: see the sick leave policy. If you are on a performance improvement plan, PTO
approval still follows the normal process.
