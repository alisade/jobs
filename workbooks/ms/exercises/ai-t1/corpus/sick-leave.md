# Sick leave

Sick leave covers your own illness, injury, medical appointments, and caring for a sick family member.
Employees receive 10 sick days per year, granted on January 1, with no carryover. A doctor's note is
required only for absences of more than three consecutive sick days.

Report a sick day to your manager before the start of your shift by phone, Teams chat, or text message,
then record it in Workday within two business days. Unused sick days are never paid out.

For illnesses that last longer than ten working days, short-term disability may apply; contact the
benefits team. Mental health days count as sick leave and need no explanation to your manager.
