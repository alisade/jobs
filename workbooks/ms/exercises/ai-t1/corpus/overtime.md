# Overtime and on-call pay

Non-exempt employees are paid overtime at 1.5 times the regular hourly rate for hours worked above 40
in a work week, and at 2 times the rate on public holidays. Overtime must be approved in advance by
your manager, except during a declared production incident.

On-call engineers receive a flat stipend of 300 dollars per on-call week plus overtime for hours
actually worked when paged. Exempt employees are not eligible for overtime but receive one day of
comp time after any on-call week with more than 4 hours of incident work.
