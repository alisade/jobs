# Parental leave

Litware offers 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave to every parent after the birth, adoption, or
foster placement of a child. Leave can be taken in one block or in up to three blocks within 12 months
of the birth or placement date.

Birthing parents also receive up to 8 weeks of medical recovery leave under short-term disability,
which runs before parental leave. Notify HR at least 30 days before the expected start date when
possible. Your role and pay grade are protected while you are on parental leave.

A phased return lets you work 60 percent of your normal hours at full pay for the first 4 weeks after
you come back. Benefits continue during leave without interruption.
