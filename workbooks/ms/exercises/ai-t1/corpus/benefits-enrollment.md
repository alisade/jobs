# Benefits enrollment

New hires must enroll in medical, dental, and vision plans within 30 days of their start date. Open
enrollment runs every November for coverage starting January 1. Outside these windows you can change
plans only after a qualifying life event such as marriage, birth, or loss of other coverage, and you
must report the event within 60 days.

Litware matches 401k contributions dollar for dollar up to 5 percent of salary, with immediate vesting.
The health savings account receives a company contribution of 750 dollars per year for individual
coverage and 1500 dollars for family coverage.
