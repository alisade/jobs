# Expense reimbursement

Submit business expenses in Concur within 30 days of the purchase. Every expense above 25 dollars
needs an itemized receipt. Expenses submitted after 90 days are rejected and are not reimbursed.

Meals with clients are reimbursable up to 75 dollars per person including tip; alcohol is reimbursable
only at client dinners approved by a director. Team meals need prior approval from your cost center
owner. Personal items, fines, parking tickets, and upgrades are never reimbursable.

Reimbursements are paid with the next payroll run after your manager approves the report. Corporate
card holders must reconcile card transactions every month.
