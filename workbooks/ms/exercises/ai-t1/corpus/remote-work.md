# Remote and hybrid work

Hybrid employees work from a Litware office at least two days per week; the anchor days are set by each
team. Fully remote roles are approved by the department head and recorded in Workday.

Working from another country is limited to 20 working days per calendar year and needs approval from
HR and the tax team before you travel, because of payroll and immigration rules. Working from another
US state for more than 30 days also needs HR approval.

Remote employees receive a one-time home office stipend of 500 dollars and a monthly internet allowance
of 40 dollars. Company laptops must use the VPN on public networks.
