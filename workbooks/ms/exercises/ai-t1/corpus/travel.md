# Business travel

Book all flights, hotels, and rental cars through the Egencia travel portal. Economy class is the
standard for flights under 6 hours; premium economy is allowed for flights over 6 hours, and business
class requires vice president approval.

Hotel nightly rates are capped by city: 250 dollars in tier one cities such as New York, London, and
San Francisco, and 180 dollars elsewhere. Per diem for meals while traveling is 70 dollars per day.

International trips need a travel risk check from the security team at least 10 days before
departure. Use the corporate card for travel; personal cards are reimbursed through expenses.
