# Office access and badges

Your badge must be visible at all times in Litware offices. Do not let anyone tailgate through a badge
door; ask them to badge in or call reception. Visitors sign in at reception and must be escorted.

A lost badge must be reported to facilities the same day so it can be deactivated. A temporary badge is
valid for one day only. Badges for data center access are issued separately and require a completed
security training and a manager request.

After-hours access between 10 pm and 6 am is logged and reviewed weekly by the security team.
