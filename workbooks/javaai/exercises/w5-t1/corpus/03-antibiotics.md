# Antibiotics and resistance

## What antibiotics target

Antibiotics act on structures that bacteria have and human cells lack, such as the peptidoglycan cell wall, bacterial ribosomes and enzymes for folate synthesis. They have no effect on viruses, which is why antibiotics do not treat influenza or the common cold.

## How resistance evolves

Resistance evolves by natural selection. Random mutations or genes acquired by horizontal gene transfer on plasmids let a few bacteria survive a dose; they multiply and the resistant strain spreads. Incomplete courses and agricultural overuse increase selection pressure.

## Stewardship

Antibiotic stewardship means prescribing the right drug at the right dose for the right duration, using narrow spectrum agents when possible, and reserving last resort drugs such as carbapenems for infections that need them.
