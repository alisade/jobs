# Welcome to Microbiology

## How this course works

Each lesson has a short reading, a tutor you can ask questions, and a quiz. You master a lesson when you answer its quiz correctly. The tutor gives hints and asks guiding questions rather than handing over quiz answers.

## Lab safety and biosafety levels

Teaching labs for this course work at biosafety level one with non pathogenic organisms. Wash hands, never pipette by mouth, and disinfect benches before and after work. Higher biosafety levels add containment for more dangerous agents.
