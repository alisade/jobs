# Describing data

## Measures of centre

The mean is the arithmetic average, the median is the middle value of ordered data, and the mode is the most frequent value. The median is robust to outliers while the mean is pulled toward extreme values in skewed distributions.

## Measures of spread

The range is the maximum minus the minimum. Variance is the average squared deviation from the mean and the standard deviation is its square root. The interquartile range covers the middle half of the data and resists outliers.

## Shape of a distribution

A distribution can be symmetric, skewed right with a long tail of large values, or skewed left. Income is a classic right skewed variable, so its mean is above its median. Histograms and box plots show shape at a glance.
