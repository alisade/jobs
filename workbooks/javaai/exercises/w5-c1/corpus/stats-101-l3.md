# Correlation and regression

## Correlation

The correlation coefficient r measures the strength and direction of a linear relationship and ranges from minus one to one. Correlation does not imply causation: a lurking confounding variable can drive both measurements.

## Least squares regression

Least squares regression fits the line that minimises the sum of squared residuals. The slope is the predicted change in the response for a one unit change in the explanatory variable, and the intercept is the prediction when the explanatory variable is zero.

## Residuals and fit

A residual is the observed value minus the predicted value. A residual plot with no pattern supports a linear model, while a curve suggests a transformation. R squared is the fraction of variation in the response explained by the model.
